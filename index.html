      FAQs

      Everything You Need to Know About Studying in Dubai

      Quick answers for students and parents.

      How can I apply to universities in Dubai for UG and PG programmes?

      Shortlist your programme, check the eligibility requirements and start your application. UG applicants need Class 10 and 12 marksheets; PG applicants need degree marksheets and certificates. Both need a valid passport and photographs.

      What are the top business management courses in Dubai for Indian students?

      Popular options include BBA, BBA (Hons), B.Com and MBA programmes, offering exposure to one of the region’s leading business hubs.

      What is the scope after a Master’s degree in Dubai for Indian students?

      A Master’s degree in Dubai can open career opportunities in business and finance, technology, consulting and management. Indian students gain global exposure, access to multinational employers and networking opportunities. Graduates can work in the UAE, return to India or pursue roles worldwide.

      Which are the best universities in Dubai for an MBA?

      Symbiosis International University Dubai and BITS Pilani Dubai are options for MBA study, with strong academics and industry exposure. Both universities offer undergraduate and postgraduate courses across business, computing, commerce, media and other disciplines.
