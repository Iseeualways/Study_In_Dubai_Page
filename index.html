      FAQs

      Frequently asked questions

      Quick answers for students and parents.

      Which colleges can I apply to through Learntech?

      Symbiosis International University Dubai and BITS Pilani Dubai, both Dubai campuses of established Indian institutions.

      Who can apply?

      Students who meet each university’s minimum marks. Symbiosis Dubai asks for 55% for Bachelor’s and 50% for Master’s. BITS Pilani Dubai asks for 60% for both. A counsellor confirms whether you qualify.

      Can I apply for a Master’s programme?

      Yes. Symbiosis Dubai offers an MBA, and BITS Pilani Dubai offers an ME in Software Systems and an MBA.

      What are the fees?

      Fees vary by college, course and level. A counsellor shares the current fee structure and a clear cost breakdown for your shortlisted course.

      What documents do I need?

      Usually your previous marksheets or transcripts, a valid passport, photographs and English proficiency proof or a waiver where the university allows it. A counsellor shares the exact checklist.

      Can I get an education loan?

      We guide you on education loan options and share a clear cost breakdown. Approval depends on the lender.

      Does Learntech guarantee admission or a visa?

      No. Admission and visas depend on the university and the relevant authorities. We guide you through each step but cannot guarantee either.
